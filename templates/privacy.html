Biometric Privacy Policy

Effective Date: October 2026 • Compliant with Global Biometric Data Standards

At VisionAttend AI, we recognize the supreme sensitivity of facial biometric data. This policy outlines how facial representations are converted, secured, and strictly protected within educational and institutional deployments.

1. Mathematical Feature Transformation

When a student enrolls their face, our computer vision model does not track emotional patterns or continuous surveillance feeds. Instead, the deep convolutional neural network extracts 128 mathematical measurements (landmarks corresponding to nodal distance ratios between cheekbones, eye contours, and jawlines).

Zero Reverse Engineering: A 128-d floating point array cannot be reverse-engineered into a high-resolution portrait photograph.

2. Data We Collect & Store

  • Academic Profile: Student Roll Number, Full Name, Department, Semester, and optional Contact details.
  • Biometric Landmark Vector: Serialized JSON array of 128 floating-point numerical embeddings.
  • Attendance Event Logs: Calendar Date, Check-in Timestamp, Biometric Confidence score, and Status.

3. Strict Non-Sharing Policy

VisionAttend AI adheres to a strict Zero Commercialization stance:

  • We never sell, license, rent, or trade student biometrics or attendance records to advertisers, third-party data brokers, or commercial AI training aggregators.
  • Data remains strictly hosted within the institution's local deployment server or dedicated private database instance.

4. Student Rights & Data Erasure

Every student retains full data autonomy under institutional privacy guidelines:

  • Right to Inspect: Students can view their complete attendance history anytime through the Student Portal.
  • Right to Erasure: Upon graduation or departure, an authorized administrator can permanently delete the student's profile, enrolled photo, and all associated face encodings with one click.

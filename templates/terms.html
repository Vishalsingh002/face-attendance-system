Last Updated: October 2026 • Educational Deployment Policy & Code of Ethics

Welcome to VisionAttend AI. By registering your biometric face embeddings, logging in as faculty or administration, or checking attendance using this platform, you agree to comply with the legal, ethical, and academic provisions outlined in this agreement.

1. Acceptance of Terms & Eligibility

This software is provided exclusively for accredited academic institutions, universities, and corporate training centers. Access to administrative controls is restricted to authorized faculty, Class Representatives (CRs), and institutional superadministrators.

2. Academic Integrity & Anti-Spoofing Policy

Students and participants are bound by institutional codes of academic honesty. The following actions constitute an explicit violation of service:

Proxy Attendance: Attempting to scan a printed photo, digital screen, or synthetic deepfake video of another student to register unauthorized attendance is strictly forbidden.
Camera Tampering: Intercepting WebRTC camera streams or feeding pre-recorded media via virtual webcams to manipulate the facial match confidence will result in automatic disciplinary suspension.
Session Hijacking: Replaying expired session codes or sharing temporary faculty broadcast tokens with non-enrolled individuals is prohibited.

3. Biometric Capture & Usage Consent

By submitting a photo or allowing the webcam to scan facial coordinates:

  • You grant the system permission to analyze facial geometric landmarks and generate 128-dimensional mathematical feature vectors.
  • You acknowledge that these mathematical vectors are utilized solely for attendance validation, audit reporting, and student performance metrics.
  • Your biometric vectors are processed locally on the institutional server with zero external third-party telemetry.

4. Role Responsibilities & Privileges

  • Superadministrators: Responsible for account security, adding authorized CRs/faculty, and performing database audits. Must securely store system credentials.
  • Faculty / CRs: Authorized to initiate live classroom scanners and manage daily active sessions. Must ensure fair scanning conditions and adequate lighting.
  • Students: Entitled to view individual percentage turnouts, attendance stamps, and examination eligibility indicators in the Student Portal.

5. System Availability & Technical Accuracy

While our ResNet-based facial recognition engine achieves over 99.3% benchmark accuracy under optimal conditions, recognition may be influenced by extreme low lighting, heavy facial occlusions, or optical distortion. In rare cases of non-recognition, students may request manual verification from the supervising instructor before the session expires.

6. Account Termination & Right to Erasure

Upon completion of an academic program, semester turnover, or formal student withdrawal, the authorized administrator will permanently purge the student's facial encoding array and stored reference image from the local filesystem and database.

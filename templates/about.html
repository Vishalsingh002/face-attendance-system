About The Platform

Pioneering Frictionless Attendance with Computer Vision

VisionAttend AI replaces traditional, error-prone manual roll calls with automated deep learning facial biometrics, restoring valuable instructional time to classrooms and universities.

The Problem We Solve

In traditional academic environments, taking attendance eats away 10 to 15 minutes of every lecture. Paper sheets lead to proxy attendance ("buddy punching"), lost records, and tedious end-of-semester manual Excel compilations.

RFID cards and biometric fingerprint scanners are easily shared or create bottleneck queues at classroom doorways.

Our Intelligent Solution

VisionAttend deploys 128-dimensional facial embeddings using pre-trained deep neural networks. Students simply walk past a webcam kiosk or verify their identity with a 2-second mobile selfie.

The system speaks the student's name aloud, confirms verification, and logs the attendance directly to SQLite databases with zero manual effort.
